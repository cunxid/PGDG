PGDG

One demonstration, then a compact recovery dataset.

This standalone page turns the paper’s Physically Grounded Sampler, Dataset Curator, and Selective Local Relabeling into a direct, tactile demo. Drag the collision sphere into the upper half of the box, tip it into the right wall, and then run PGDG on the recorded motion to see Bezier control-point sampling, frontier-aware curation, diversity selection, local relabeling, and the final dataset summary.

How To Use
Move the sphere to the top-left of the box, push across the roofline toward the wall, and hold long enough for the box to settle near 90 degrees. Use Seeded demo if you want a guaranteed successful reference before sampling.

Success predicate: the box must reach the wall and settle near a 90 degree tip. The mouse sphere is the only actuator; the rest of the page is generated from that single recorded demonstration.

Generated Output

Generated Trajectory Ensemble

This direct view overlays the recorded demo with all successful generated trajectories so the generated distribution is visible at a glance.

The mechanics are intentionally lightweight so the whole experience stays inside one HTML file, but the control flow mirrors the paper: a single demonstration becomes a sampling distribution over Bezier control points, successful trajectories are scored in an adaptive recovery tube, DPP-style diversity selection removes redundancy, and CEM-style relabeling repairs a few risky supervision points.
